# Fixes

Bugs found by using the app, most of them during the demo on Leon's MacBook (2026-09-28). Work top to bottom, tick items off, and describe the fix in one line under the item. Every fix starts by reproducing the bug the way a user meets it.

- [x] **Scenario switch shows the wrong scenario.** In Scenarios, switching from Scenario 1 to Scenario 2 leaves every input under it showing the values of Scenario 1. The switch does not work.
      Fixed: the heating inputs, construction choices and GEG check under the Energy panel switch now show the chosen scenario, and edits go into it (the built-in Renovated variant is read only).
      The user's actual bug was in the Scenarios tab: choosing Scenario 2 in the table left the editor below showing Scenario 1 first, because it listed every scenario. Now the editor shows only the scenario chosen in the table, and a rename is kept with a Save button that appears once the name changes.
- [x] **A photo lands on the wrong building.** In the demo: one photo went through "Apply to 3D model" and was applied. Then another building was chosen on the 2D map, its photo was straightened by the AI and its openings drawn, and "Apply to 3D model" put it on the old building. A total disaster in the presentation. The editor must never mix two buildings. Fix it with projects: every building gets its own project, and applying a photo goes into the project of the building it was taken of (see "Projects per building" in IDEAS.md).
      Fixed: every building from the map has its own project; switching leaves the old project first (the address carried `?project=A` to the next building, and the editor then joined A). Manual check pending.
- [x] **Photo editor flashes between two images (kY).** With kY and its photo, after the AI finished, the photo editor flashed very fast between two images and did not show the straightened image correctly. Likely cause: the image size differs from the photo editor size and the layout keeps resizing. Fix: give the photo editor a fixed width that does not depend on the image.
      Fixed: one fixed dialog width; the wall column no longer sizes itself from the image. Manual check pending.
- [ ] **Corners of the chosen wall part.** On kY the middle wall of the street facade was chosen in the 3D map. The AI must know which part of the facade was chosen and find the corners of that middle wall in the photo, not of the whole facade.
      Not fixed yet: a first version was tested on kY and reverted, it cut in the wrong place. The findings and the real plan are in TODO.md, Part 7 X.
- [x] **Plan view lines are invisible (2026-09-29).** In the floor plan view only the arrowheads showed: no dimension lines, extension lines, door swings or window frames. The room label also covered the room's width by height.
      Fixed: the plan group is mirrored (scale z = -1), which flips which side of a mesh counts as front, and drei's fat lines are meshes built in screen space, so every one was culled; this hit every line in the editor (measure tool, drawing previews, pipes, thermal bridges, compass, sun path). All of them now go through `src/scene/Line.tsx`, which draws both sides. The 3D room label is hidden in the plan view, where the plan's own stamp shows name, area and size. Manual check pending.
- [x] **The first SAM 3 detection after a start is slow (2026-09-29).** It took 16.6 s instead of under 2 s. SAM 3 had not changed: the service had just been restarted, and the first pass through the model after a start sets up the graphics card (4.1 s against 0.7 s for "find the wall"), on top of waiting for the model to load.
      Fixed: the service runs one warm-up pass at startup before it says it is ready, so the first photo takes 0.9 s like every later one. Startup takes about 6 s longer.
- [x] **Work turns up in a new project and is saved without asking (2026-09-29).** Leaving the editor asked nothing, the Projects page said "No projects yet", and a new project of the same house showed the floor plan applied before.
      Fixed: a project exists only once it is saved (Save in the project bar, Ctrl+S); leaving unsaved work asks Save, Don't save or Cancel; a new project always starts from a fresh model with no photos, floor plans or undo of another; each project keeps its own photos and floor plan images. See DECISIONS.md. Manual check pending.
- [x] **An interior wall cannot be selected, so it cannot be resized (2026-09-29).** With the select tool a click on an interior wall did nothing visible, and its grips never showed.
      Fixed: the wall selected itself on pointer up, but the click that follows fell through to the room under it (rooms reach under their walls) and selected the room instead; a room now takes a click only when its floor is what was hit. In the floor plan an interior wall also picks along a strip 8 px to each side, since 10 cm is a few pixels. Manual check pending.
- [x] **Interior walls run into the exterior walls, a picked wall runs through several rooms, and its grips do not move (2026-09-29).**
      Fixed: walls are drawn only where they are seen (between the faces they meet, straight through joints, filled at corners); a wall is the piece between two joints, so a pick lights up the wall of that room; a picked wall moves sideways by dragging it instead of by its ends. See DECISIONS.md. Manual check pending.
- [x] **Interior walls poke through the exterior walls in 3D (2026-09-29).** The plan was fixed, the 3D model still built each interior wall over its whole stored length.
      Fixed: the 3D solid is built over the same range the plan draws (between the faces it meets, straight through joints, filled at corners). Only the mesh is cut: the stored walls, openings, rooms, areas and energy figures are unchanged, since nothing that measures uses the solid. Manual check pending.
